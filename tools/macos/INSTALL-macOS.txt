Maquina for macOS - CLAP + VST3, universal (Intel + Apple Silicon), macOS 10.15 or later

What the plugins are: README.md. Let an AI coding agent do it all: INSTALL-with-AI.md.

The plugins are signed ad hoc, not by a registered Apple developer, so macOS blocks
them until their download "quarantine" is cleared. All three ways below do that.
Quit your DAW (Bitwig, Live, Reaper...) first: a running DAW keeps the old plugins.


A. Terminal (recommended: no security dialogs)

  1. Open the .dmg (double-click it, e.g. ~/Downloads/Maquina-<version>-macOS.dmg).
     It appears as /Volumes/Maquina <version>.
  2. Open Terminal: Cmd+Space, type Terminal, Return.
  3. Paste this line and press Return:

       sh /Volumes/Maquina*/"Install Maquina.command"

     It copies every plugin to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP and VST3, replaces older
     Maquina versions, clears the quarantine and lists what it installed.
     (Only one Maquina disk image may be open; eject older ones first.)
  4. Start your DAW and rescan plugins.

  The same, step by step, if you want to see each one:

       mkdir -p ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
       rm -rf ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Maquina*.clap ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Maquina*.vst3
       cp -R /Volumes/Maquina*/CLAP/Maquina*.clap ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
       cp -R /Volumes/Maquina*/VST3/Maquina*.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Maquina*.clap
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Maquina*.vst3

  (The rm line removes old Maquina plugins only; your presets are elsewhere and stay.)


B. Double-click the installer

  1. Open the .dmg and double-click "Install Maquina.command".
  2. macOS says it can't verify the developer. Click Done (or Cancel), then:
     - macOS 15 Sequoia and later: System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
       click "Open Anyway" next to "Install Maquina.command", confirm with your password.
     - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click "Install Maquina.command" > Open > Open.
  3. A Terminal window lists the installed plugins. Press Return to close it.
  4. Start your DAW and rescan plugins.


C. By hand in Finder

  1. Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/ (create the folders
     CLAP and VST3 there if they are missing).
  2. Drag the .clap bundles from the disk image's CLAP folder into CLAP, and the .vst3
     bundles from its VST3 folder into VST3. Replace older ones.
  3. Clear the quarantine once, in Terminal:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Maquina*.clap
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Maquina*.vst3

     Without this the DAW does not list the plugins, or macOS says they "can't be
     opened" / "are damaged".


In your DAW

  Bitwig Studio: Settings > Locations > Plug-ins, rescan. CLAP and VST3 both work.
  Ableton Live: VST3 only. Settings > Plug-Ins: turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System
  Folders", then Rescan (hold Option for a full rescan). Browser > Plug-Ins > Maquina.
  Reaper: Settings > Plug-ins > VST / CLAP, "Re-scan".
  Logic and GarageBand need AU plugins and can't load Maquina.

User presets are saved in ~/Library/Application Support/Maquina/presets/<plugin>/.

Uninstall (Terminal):

  rm -rf ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Maquina*.clap ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Maquina*.vst3

  Your presets and editor settings, only if you want them gone too:

  rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/Maquina
